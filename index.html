Why The Loop

A place to actually go deeper into Brazilian Zouk Music.

You hear a track on the floor that hits you, and then you can't find it anywhere. You've got questions about gear or mixing and nobody to ask. You want to learn more about the craft of DJing. The Loop is the room for all of that — tracks, mixing, gear, "what is this song?!", and people who care about it as much as you do.

A month in or ten years deep, dancer or DJ, there's a seat here.

Who we are

DJs building for DJs.

We're three Brazilian Zouk DJs from three countries, and we built the thing we kept wishing existed when we were starting. A place for anyone who loves this music — dancers, community leaders, DJs — to share tracks, ask questions, and grow together. No gatekeeping, no competition. Just the crew we all wanted.

DJ Kron

Czech Republic

DJ Kron is a Brazilian Zouk DJ, dancer, and teacher from the Czech Republic. Known for deep, emotional sets that challenge dancers' creativity and build musical flow, he's been shaping Zouk floors across Europe for over 8 years. A lifelong music lover who believes the right track at the right moment can change a room.

DJ Sprenk

USA

DJ Sprenk is a Brazilian Zouk DJ based in Cambridge, MA. Originally a musician, his approach to DJing is rooted in music theory and emotional storytelling — known across North America for genre-blending arcs and a healthy dose of nerdy joy.

DJ Xearo

Netherlands

DJ Xearo is a DJ, musical director, and instructor working internationally within the Brazilian Zouk community. Based in Amsterdam and playing top marathons and festivals across Europe since 2016, his focus is always the same: right music, right moment.

FAQ

Good questions.

How does it work?

The Loop is a free Discord (opens in new tab) community. Create an account, get access to channels for sharing music, getting feedback, and connecting with Brazilian Zouk DJs and lovers around the world.

Is it free?

Yes. The community is free and always will be. If you want structured, guided learning, The Loop Academy is a separate paid program — but the community itself costs nothing.

What if I'm new to DJing?

Perfect. The Loop is built for every level. And the Academy's first month is about listening and music identity — no gear needed — so you can start before you ever touch a controller.

What if I'm already experienced?

There's plenty here for you too. Trade tracks, share knowledge, get a second ear, find your next gig, and help shape the room. The deeper you go, the more you get.

What if I just love the music and don't DJ?

You're welcome here. Some of our members don't DJ at all — they come to discover music, talk about it, and be around people who love Brazilian Zouk as much as they do.

What's The Loop Academy?

A 12-month guided DJ program in small cohorts, opening July 2026. Takes you from music discovery to performing live, with feedback at every stage.
